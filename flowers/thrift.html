Thrift (Sea Pink)

海石竹 · 海石竹 · 海石竹 · Armérie maritime · Clavelina de mar

Armeria maritima

Pink pom-poms on bare salty cliffs. The whole island's coast turns pink in May! Old Irish three-pence coins had thrift on the back.

Height
25 cm
Blooms
Apr–Aug
Family
Plumbago (Plumbaginaceae)
Latin name
Armeria maritima
Where to find
  • Sea cliffs
  • Cliffs of Moher (Clare)
  • Salt marshes

Where you can find Thrift (Sea Pink) in Ireland

  • Cliffs of Moher — County Clare: Towering Atlantic sea cliffs covered in pink thrift cushions and white sea campion in May and June. The wild salty wind shapes everything that grows here.
  • Howth Head — Dublin: A rocky peninsula at the edge of Dublin city. Heather and gorse on the cliff tops, bluebells in the woods in spring, and great views of the sea below.

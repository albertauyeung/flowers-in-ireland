Yellow Horned-poppy

黃花海罌粟 · 黄花海罂粟 · 黃花海罌粟 · Glaucienne jaune · Adormidera marina

Glaucium flavum

A bright yellow poppy of pebbly beaches, with grey-green crinkly leaves. After flowering it grows ridiculously long curved seed-pods like cow horns!

⚠️ Poisonous — do not pick or taste this plant.

Height
60 cm
Blooms
Jun–Sep
Family
Poppy (Papaveraceae)
Latin name
Glaucium flavum
Where to find
  • Shingle beaches
  • Sea cliffs
  • Coastal sand

Where you can find Yellow Horned-poppy in Ireland

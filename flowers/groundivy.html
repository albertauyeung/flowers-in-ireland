Ground Ivy

連錢草 · 连钱草 · 連錢草 · Lierre terrestre · Hiedra terrestre

Glechoma hederacea

Not really an ivy — but a creeping mint with little blue-violet hooded flowers. People used to brew it like beer before they used hops!

Height
20 cm
Blooms
Mar–Jun
Family
Mint (Lamiaceae)
Latin name
Glechoma hederacea
Where to find
  • Hedge banks
  • Damp meadows
  • Garden corners

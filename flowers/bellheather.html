Bell Heather

灰歐石楠 · 灰欧石楠 · 灰歐石楠 · Bruyère cendrée · Brezo ceniciento

Erica cinerea

Bigger, more bell-shaped flowers than common heather, in a richer purple-pink. Loves dry rocky ground, while ling prefers boggy ground.

Height
60 cm
Blooms
Jun–Sep
Family
Heath (Ericaceae)
Latin name
Erica cinerea
Where to find
  • Dry heaths
  • Rocky moors
  • Cliff tops

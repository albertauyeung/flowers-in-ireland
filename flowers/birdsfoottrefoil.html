Common Bird's-foot-trefoil

百脈根 · 百脉根 · 百脈根 · Lotier corniculé · Loto corniculado

Lotus corniculatus

Little yellow and orange flowers in tight clusters. After flowering, the seed pods spread out like the toes of a bird's foot — that's how it got its name!

Height
25 cm
Blooms

Where you can find Common Bird's-foot-trefoil in Ireland

  • The Burren — County Clare: A magical limestone landscape on the west coast — over 70% of Ireland's native flowers grow here, including 24 of 28 native orchids. Arctic, alpine and Mediterranean plants live side by side!
  • Cliffs of Moher — County Clare: Towering Atlantic sea cliffs covered in pink thrift cushions and white sea campion in May and June. The wild salty wind shapes everything that grows here.
  • Howth Head — Dublin: A rocky peninsula at the edge of Dublin city. Heather and gorse on the cliff tops, bluebells in the woods in spring, and great views of the sea below.

Lords-and-Ladies (Cuckoo Pint)

斑葉疆南星 · 斑叶疆南星 · 斑葉疆南星 · Arum tacheté · Aro manchado

Arum maculatum

A weird hooded green flower with a dark purple finger inside that smells of rotten meat — to fool flies into pollinating it! In autumn, bright orange-red berries.

⚠️ Poisonous — do not pick or taste this plant.

Height
30 cm
Blooms
Apr–May
Family
Arum (Araceae)
Latin name
Arum maculatum
Where to find
  • Damp shady woods
  • Hedgerow shade
  • Old churchyards

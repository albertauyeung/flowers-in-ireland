Yarrow

蓍草 · 蓍草 · 蓍草 · Achillée millefeuille · Milenrama

Achillea millefolium

Flat clusters of tiny white (sometimes pink) flowers above feathery leaves. Named after the Greek hero Achilles, who used it to heal soldiers' wounds!

Height
60 cm
Blooms
Jun–Oct
Family
Daisy (Asteraceae)
Latin name
Achillea millefolium
Where to find
  • Roadside verges
  • Lawns and pasture
  • Old meadows

Where you can find Yarrow in Ireland

  • Phoenix Park — Dublin: One of Europe's biggest city parks. Lawns full of daisies and dandelions, hedgerows of hawthorn and blackthorn, and old trees clothed in ivy. A great first place to learn flowers.

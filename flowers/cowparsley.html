Cow Parsley

峨參 · 峨参 · 峨參 · Cerfeuil sauvage · Perifollo verde

Anthriscus sylvestris

Frothy white "lace" lining country lanes in May, like the roadside is dressed for a wedding! Don't confuse it with the (very poisonous) hemlock.

Height
130 cm
Blooms
Apr–Jun
Family
Carrot (Apiaceae)
Latin name
Anthriscus sylvestris
Where to find
  • Roadside verges
  • Country lanes
  • Hedge banks

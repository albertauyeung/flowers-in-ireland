Cross-leaved Heath

十字葉歐石楠 · 十字叶欧石楠 · 十字葉歐石楠 · Bruyère à quatre angles · Brezo de turbera

Erica tetralix

Pretty pink bell-flowers in tight nodding clusters, growing on Ireland's wet bogs. The leaves stick out in fours, making a tiny cross around the stem.

Height
50 cm
Blooms
Jun–Sep
Family
Heath (Ericaceae)
Latin name
Erica tetralix
Where to find
  • Wet bogs
  • Connemara (Galway)
  • Wicklow blanket bog

Where you can find Cross-leaved Heath in Ireland

  • Connemara National Park — County Galway: Wild Atlantic mountains, blanket bogs and twinkling lakes. Look for purple heather and yellow bog asphodel in summer, and Atlantic-fringe plants like sundews and butterworts.
  • Wild Nephin National Park — County Mayo: A vast wild bog and mountain wilderness on the west coast. One of Europe's largest blanket bogs — perfect for finding sundews, butterworts and bog cotton.

Geranium robertianum

Small bright pink flowers and red-tinged ferny leaves. Crush a leaf — it has a strong, sharp smell some people don't like!

Height
35 cm
Blooms
Apr–Oct
Family
Geranium (Geraniaceae)
Latin name

  • Hedgerows

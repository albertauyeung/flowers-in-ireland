Common Ragwort

千里光 · 千里光 · 千里光 · Séneçon jacobée · Hierba de Santiago

Jacobaea vulgaris

Tall flat-topped clusters of bright yellow flowers — and the favourite food of the stripy black-and-orange cinnabar moth caterpillar. Don't pick: it's harmful to horses and people.

⚠️ Poisonous — do not pick or taste this plant.

Height
100 cm

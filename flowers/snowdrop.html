Snowdrop

雪花蓮 · 雪花莲 · 雪花蓮 · Perce-neige · Campanilla de invierno

Galanthus nivalis

The very first flower of the year! Tiny white drops that push up through frosty soil in January — a sign that spring is on its way.

Height
18 cm
Blooms
Jan–Mar
Family
Amaryllis (Amaryllidaceae)
Latin name
Galanthus nivalis
Where to find
  • Old gardens and demesnes
  • Woodland edges
  • Churchyards

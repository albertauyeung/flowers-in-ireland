Great Willowherb

毛柳葉菜 · 毛柳叶菜 · 毛柳葉菜 · Épilobe hirsute · Epilobio peludo

Epilobium hirsutum

Tall fuzzy plant with big purple-pink four-petalled flowers along streams. Old country name: "codlins-and-cream" — apparently it smelled like apples cooked in cream!

Height
180 cm
Blooms
Jul–Sep
Family
Evening Primrose (Onagraceae)
Latin name
Epilobium hirsutum
Where to find
  • River banks
  • Wet ditches
  • Pond edges

Red Clover

紅三葉草 · 红三叶草 · 紅三葉草 · Trèfle des prés · Trébol rojo

Trifolium pratense

Soft round purple-pink flower-heads on bushy plants. Bumblebees with long tongues are the star pollinators — short-tongued bees can't reach the nectar!

Height
40 cm
Blooms
May–Sep
Family
Pea (Fabaceae)
Latin name
Trifolium pratense
Where to find
  • Meadows
  • Roadside verges
  • Pasture

Where you can find Red Clover in Ireland

  • Phoenix Park — Dublin: One of Europe's biggest city parks. Lawns full of daisies and dandelions, hedgerows of hawthorn and blackthorn, and old trees clothed in ivy. A great first place to learn flowers.

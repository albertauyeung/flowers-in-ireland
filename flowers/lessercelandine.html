Lesser Celandine

榕葉毛茛 · 榕叶毛茛 · 榕葉毛茛 · Ficaire (renoncule ficaire) · Celidonia menor

Ficaria verna

A starry yellow flower that opens in early spring sunshine and closes when the sky turns grey. The poet Wordsworth wrote a poem just for it!

Height
10 cm
Blooms
Feb–May
Family
Buttercup (Ranunculaceae)
Latin name
Ficaria verna
Where to find
  • Woodland floors
  • Hedge banks
  • Damp shady ground

Where you can find Lesser Celandine in Ireland

  • Killarney National Park — County Kerry: Ancient oak woods, rushing streams and big lakes. In April and May the woodland floor is carpeted with bluebells, wild garlic and wood anemones — pure magic!

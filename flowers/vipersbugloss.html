Viper's Bugloss

藍薊 · 蓝蓟 · 藍薊 · Vipérine commune · Viborera

Echium vulgare

Tall spikes of brilliant blue flowers with red stamens that stick out like little snake tongues. People once thought it cured snake bites!

80 cm

Family

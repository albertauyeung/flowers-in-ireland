Bloody Cranesbill

血紅老鸛草 · 血红老鹳草 · 血紅老鸛草 · Géranium sanguin · Geranio sangriento

Geranium sanguineum

Big magenta flowers carpet the Burren limestone in summer. The Latin name means "blood-red". Ireland's national flower? Definitely a strong contender!

Height
30 cm
Blooms
Jun–Aug
Family
Geranium (Geraniaceae)
Latin name
Geranium sanguineum
Where to find

  • Limestone grassland

Where you can find Bloody Cranesbill in Ireland

  • The Burren — County Clare: A magical limestone landscape on the west coast — over 70% of Ireland's native flowers grow here, including 24 of 28 native orchids. Arctic, alpine and Mediterranean plants live side by side!

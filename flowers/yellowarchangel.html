Yellow Archangel

黃花野芝麻 · 黄花野芝麻 · 黃花野芝麻 · Lamier jaune · Ortiga amarilla

Lamium galeobdolon

Whorls of bright yellow hooded flowers shoot up around square stems in May. Like a dead-nettle, it looks like nettle but doesn't sting — a friend with no bite.

Height
60 cm
Blooms
May–Jun
Family
Mint (Lamiaceae)
Latin name
Lamium galeobdolon
Where to find
  • Old woodland floors
  • Hedge banks
  • Shady damp ground

Where you can find Yellow Archangel in Ireland

  • Killarney National Park — County Kerry: Ancient oak woods, rushing streams and big lakes. In April and May the woodland floor is carpeted with bluebells, wild garlic and wood anemones — pure magic!

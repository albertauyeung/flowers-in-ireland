Round-leaved Sundew

圓葉茅膏菜 · 圆叶茅膏菜 · 圓葉茅膏菜 · Rossolis à feuilles rondes · Rocío del sol

Drosera rotundifolia

Another carnivorous plant! Round red leaves are covered with sticky hairs that glisten like dewdrops. Insects get stuck — and then eaten!

Where you can find Round-leaved Sundew in Ireland

  • Connemara National Park — County Galway: Wild Atlantic mountains, blanket bogs and twinkling lakes. Look for purple heather and yellow bog asphodel in summer, and Atlantic-fringe plants like sundews and butterworts.
  • Wicklow Mountains National Park — County Wicklow: Ireland's largest national park, just south of Dublin. Heather-clad mountains, deep valleys with foxgloves and bog-loving sundews and butterworts.
  • Wild Nephin National Park — County Mayo: A vast wild bog and mountain wilderness on the west coast. One of Europe's largest blanket bogs — perfect for finding sundews, butterworts and bog cotton.
